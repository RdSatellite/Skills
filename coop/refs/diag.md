# !coop diag

> alias: `!diag`

## Description

The part in question has a problem.

Gather the relevant context, diagnose the likely cause, and provide a concrete solution suggestion.

Do not implement the solution. The purpose is to understant **why** the problem occurs and **how** it could be fixed.

## Hint

- Restricted handling scope, stick to the task.
- Don't do any modification but just report them in the chat window.
